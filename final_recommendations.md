# Final Recommendations

The demonstration dataset is synthetic and exists solely to show the analytical workflow.
Do not present its numerical findings as real CountryEdu company figures.

1. Prioritize the highest-cost issue categories identified by the Pareto analysis.
2. Automate repetitive, high-volume, low-complexity ticket types.
3. Reduce unnecessary escalations by improving first-line routing and knowledge articles.
4. Use escalation-risk scoring to route complex tickets early.
5. Rebalance engineering capacity using workload and resolution-hour metrics.
6. Track CSAT alongside cost so cost cutting does not damage customer experience.
7. Handle missing data with variable-specific imputation and missingness flags.
8. Use streaming infrastructure for 50K events/sec and pre-computed features for <5-second prediction.
9. Set a governance rule that the 20% savings target must be validated against baseline cost.
10. Review the KPI dashboard weekly and run a monthly model/data-quality review.

Illustrative savings bridge:
- Automation: 8 percentage points
- Escalation reduction: 5 percentage points
- Engineer reallocation: 4 percentage points
- Resolution-time improvement: 3 percentage points
Total target: 20 percentage points

These percentages are planning targets, not measured results.
